== Description ==


[Poptics](https://aethonic.com/poptics/) is a lightweight and powerful [popup builder for WordPress](https://aethonic.com/poptics/) that helps you create [high-converting popups](https://aethonic.com/how-poptics-uses-popups-a-complete-tutorial-guide/), [exit-intent popups](https://docs.aethonic.com/docs/getting-started/display-controls/how-to-set-audience-and-targeting/), [email optins](https://docs.aethonic.com/docs/getting-started/campaign-types/how-to-create-a-lightbox-popup-campaign/), and [WooCommerce popups](https://docs.aethonic.com/docs/getting-started/integrations/woocommerce/) without writing code. It is made for site owners who want simple tools to grow leads, subscribers and sales without slowing down their website.

With Poptics, you can build any type of popup, including email popup, **[lightbox popup](https://aethonic.com/how-to-add-lightbox-popups/), form popup or [multi-step forms](https://aethonic.com/how-to-add-multi-step-popup-forms/), subscription popup, newsletter popup, [conversion popup](https://aethonic.com/discount-popup) or marketing popup** using an easy **drag-and-drop editor**. Every popup is responsive, fast and designed to convert visitors into customers.

Poptics also includes smart triggers like **exit intent, time delay, scroll depth, click triggers and page-level targeting**. These help you show the right popup at the right time, whether it’s a lead generation popup, a WooCommerce popup or a simple banner message.

For eCommerce store owners, Poptics offers **[eCommerce popups](https://aethonic.com/ecommerce-popups/) for upsell, cross-sell, cart recovery and product promotions**. It also works as an **AI popup builder**, helping you generate messages and ideas that match your goals. This makes it easier for beginners and busy site owners to launch campaigns quickly.

With **built-in analytics, A/B testing, audience targeting, scheduling**, Poptics comes with a suite of industry-based **pre-designed popup templates** that specifically grab the attention of all types of audiences and convert them into loyal customers.

== Installation ==

### Minimum Requirements

- WordPress version 5.2 or higher (tested up to 6.7)
- PHP version 7.3 or higher

### Automatic Installation

Installing Poptics automatically is simple because WordPress handles everything.

- Log in to your WordPress dashboard.
- Go to “**Plugins”** on the left menu and click “**Add New”**.
- In the search bar, type “**Poptics**.”
- Click “**Install Now”** next to the Poptics plugin.
- After the installation, click “**Activate”** to enable the plugin.

### Manual Installation

You can also install Poptics manually by downloading the plugin and uploading it to your website.

- Download and unzip the Poptics plugin.
- Use an FTP client or CPanel to upload the plugin folder to **wp-content/plugins/** in your WordPress directory.
- Go to **Plugins** in your WordPress dashboard and activate the plugin from the list.

### Upgrading

Poptics updates automatically, but if you’ve customized the plugin, make a backup before updating. Sometimes, updating can cause permalink issues.

To fix permalink issues:

- Go to “**Settings** > **Permalinks”** in WordPress.
- Click “**Save Changes”** to refresh the settings.
